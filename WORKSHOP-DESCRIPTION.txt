Remote Resurrection - Scroll Menu

Right-click a Resurrection Scroll and choose "Revive [character name]" to bring a dead teammate
back beside the caster, even when their body is on the other side of the current map.

FEATURES
- Adds a named list of eligible dead party members to the scroll's right-click menu.
- Revives the selected teammate near the caster, with no distance or line-of-sight requirement.
- Uses one Resurrection Scroll and 3 AP during combat; restores 20% vitality.
- Shows a disabled message when no eligible teammates are dead.
- Keeps the original Cast skill action available.

REQUIRES
Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition (Windows).
Norbyte's Script Extender v60 or later on the host and every client using the menu:
https://github.com/Norbyte/ositools/releases

HOW TO USE
Enable the mod. Select the caster, put a Resurrection Scroll in that character's inventory,
right-click it, and choose "Revive [name]". Stand in a clear area for the landing position.

SCOPE
Keyboard and mouse, original Story campaign. Same loaded map/region only; no travel back to
previous acts. NPCs, summons and story-blocked resurrections are excluded. The custom action
uses fixed AP and health values; talent discounts/bonuses are not reproduced. Controller and
split-screen interfaces are not supported in this initial build.

INITIAL BUILD STATUS

menu and resurrection beside the caster. Remote-client co-op and combat testing remain pending. The first Workshop upload should be private for testing.
